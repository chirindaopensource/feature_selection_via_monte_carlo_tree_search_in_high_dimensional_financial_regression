## Key callables, Tasks 1 to 36

This discussion covers the 36 final task orchestrators of `feature_selection_via_monte_carlo_tree_search_in_high_dimensional_financial_regression_draft.ipynb`, in notebook task order. Thirty-two are named `run_task_1` to `run_task_36`; the orchestrators of Tasks 17, 22, 25 and 27 are `terminal_fit_task_17`, `run_gnrpa_task_22`, `run_task_25_baselines` and `run_lasso_sparsity_stress_task_27`. Python implementation author: **CS Chirinda**. The orchestrators' signatures and documented contracts, the docstrings of the stage callables they compose, and the notebook's declared constants establish behaviour; the paper establishes the methodological standard. Internal helpers are accounted for inside their parent task rather than counted as additional callables. The notebook was not executed for this document.

Notation: session $t$, adjusted NASDAQ-100 close $P_t$, feature set $\mathcal{F}=\lbrace f_1,\dots,f_p\rbrace$, subset $S$, state $s_t=(f_{\mathrm{last}},S_t)$, policy table $W$, heuristic bias $\beta$, temperature $\tau$, and $M$ ensemble runs. Paper equations are numbered as in `latex_context.txt`: (1) softmax policy; (2) policy update; (3) target; (4) $L_0$ problem; (5) action set; (6) stabilized OLS; (7) redundancy; (8) feature bias; (9) stopping bias; (10) lexicographic reward; (11) MaxVIF; (12) ensemble linearity.

Data boundary: manuscript figures below are comparison targets, not results reported by this document. The Yahoo Finance inputs are not of institutional grade and stand in for Bloomberg or DataBento data. Two manuscript counts are recorded as documented discrepancies rather than enforced as failures: $T=6,260$ is not reproducible from the exchange calendars (6,600 sessions over the stated span), and $p=1,072$ cannot be constructed from the disclosed feature families. All $R^2$ figures are explanatory and contemporaneous, never predictive. Task numbers identify notebook cells, not runtime calls: Task 34 drives Tasks 1–33, and Tasks 9–23 execute inside the search stage.

### Task 1 — `run_task_1`

- **Inputs**: `config`, including `raw_input_schemas`; `panels`, the four ingested panels; `output_dir`.
- **Process**: runs the schema validator, the manuscript numeric validator and the interpretive validator in that order. Step 1 writes `schema_audit_<stamp>.json` and raises on a structural failure; Steps 2 and 3 never raise.
- **Outputs**: `status`, `stamp`, `schema`, `numeric_domain`, `interpretive`, `artifact_path`.
- **Transformation**: raw panels and the configuration become a merged audit whose status is the maximum severity over `pass < warn < fail`. The file `task_1_audit_<stamp>.json` is written and re-read before any Step 2 or 3 failure is raised.
- **Research role**: guards the paper's Section 3.1 task definition, its Section 4.1 partition and the Table 2 and Equation (6) constants before any computation.
- **Equations**: the stabilizer of Equation (6) and the partition invariants that are checked:

$$
\lambda=10^{-5},\qquad \mathrm{split}=2023\text{-}12\text{-}31,\qquad V=562,\qquad T_{\mathrm{test}}=562,\qquad W\in\lbrace1890,1890,1000\rbrace.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: both stamps equal the maximum `source_fetched_at_utc` across the panels, so artifact names are deterministic. A persisted status that differs from the merged status raises `RuntimeError`.

### Task 2 — `run_task_2`

- **Inputs**: `calendar`, `yahoo_panels`, `fred_panel`, `factor_panel`, `config`, `output_dir`.
- **Process**: resolves the FRED fill policy, the release-lag table and the burn-in once, into a private copy of the preprocessing block. It then prunes the panels, audits missing values and builds the value-domain report.
- **Outputs**: `pruned`, `audited`, `value_domain`, `status` (`pass`, `log` or `fail`).
- **Transformation**: duplicates are removed (the open row is retained) and session existence is enforced. Missing values are audited under `forward_fill_after_release_lag`, and numeric columns are fingerprinted without clipping.
- **Research role**: implements the Section 3.1 data discipline for market, macroeconomic and factor sources.
- **Equations**: a FRED value for series $j$ with release lag $\ell_j$ becomes visible only from its availability date:

$$
\tau_{j}(d)=d+\ell_j,\qquad x_{j,t}\ \text{visible at}\ t\iff\tau_j(d)\le t.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: this stage reports on the native, unreindexed FRED and factor frames. The as-of join and the post-join NaN abort belong to Task 7. Winsorization must be `None`, because Step 3 is a report, not an intervention.

### Task 3 — `run_task_3`

- **Outputs**: `external`, `half_day`, `cardinality`, `report_path`.
- **Transformation**: an ingested calendar becomes a verified session set with one-sided differences, half-day relations and disputed dates.
- **Research role**: supports the Section 3.1 sample of daily sessions from January 2000 to March 2026 on one coherent timestamp index.
- **Equations**: the external set is a union, not an intersection, and half-days must be a subset of open sessions:

$$
S_{\mathrm{ext}}=S_{\mathrm{NASDAQ}}\cup S_{\mathrm{NYSE}}\cup S_{\mathrm{CBOE}},\qquad \mathcal{H}\subseteq\mathcal{O}.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: external disagreement is a warning. A count different from $T$ is recorded in `t_discrepancy`, not treated as a blocker; other cardinality blockers still raise after the report is on disk.

r_t=\frac{P_t-P_{t-1}}{P_{t-1}},\qquad Y_t=\sum_{k=0}^{4}r_{t-k}.

- **Implementation distinction**: the declared `burn_in_rows` is four incomplete target rows; the effective calendar burn-in is five, because $r_0$ is undefined. Both counts are asserted and audited.

### Task 5 — `run_task_5`

$$
\bar x_{t}^{(w)}=\frac{1}{w}\sum_{k=0}^{w-1}x_{t-k},\quad w\in\mathcal{W},\qquad 1072=425+133+111+403.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: the 403-feature remainder is unspecified by the manuscript. An indicator shortfall is reported per family, not raised; Task 6 closes it. The target-window barrier lags the NDX and MKT base series by $h$ sessions before construction.

\mathrm{drop}(f)\iff f\ \text{is NDX-derived}\ \wedge\ \mathrm{lookback}(f)<5,\qquad \mathcal{W}_t=\lbrace t-h+1,\dots,t\rbrace.

- **Research role**: implements the deliberate contemporaneous alignment of Section 3.1, which the paper ties to same-day volatility, credit and equity co-movement.

$$
X\in\mathbb{R}^{T\times p},\qquad b=\max(b_X,b_Y).
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: in the orchestrated pipeline the aligned series are consumed upstream by Task 5, so Steps 1 and 2 here re-derive them deterministically. The default factor fill tolerance is five sessions.

### Task 8 — `run_task_8`

- **Inputs**: `design_matrix`, `config`; optional `calendar`, `output_dir`.
- **Process**: splits by date, builds the validation and training windows for each configuration, builds the walk-forward folds and the fold factory, and checks every post-condition.
- **Outputs**: `partition`, `search_environment`, `folds`, `folds_by_configuration`, `fold_factory`, `fold_assumption`, `calendar_slices`.
- **Transformation**: one matrix becomes pre-2024 and test blocks, three training and validation pairs, and pairwise-disjoint folds.
- **Research role**: implements Section 4.1: a 562-session test block, a 562-session validation window immediately before it, and three non-overlapping pre-2024 folds.
- **Equations**: with test start $t_0$, validation length $V=562$ and training width $W_c$:

$$
\mathcal{V}=[t_0-V,t_0),\qquad \mathcal{T}_c=[t_0-V-W_c,t_0-V),\qquad W_c\in\lbrace1890,1890,1000\rbrace.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: the fold placement is a documented interpretation of "non-overlapping", recorded in `fold_assumption`. The factory rebuilds training starts for a trial window while the validation boundaries stay frozen.

### Task 9 — `run_task_9`

- **Inputs**: `p`, `K_max`, `max_steps`, `evaluate_r2`, `evaluate_vif`; keyword `action_sampler`.
- **Process**: starts at the empty state and records every state and action, checking capacity and stop before each sampler call.
- **Outputs**: `states`, `actions`, `terminal`, `reward`.
- **Transformation**: a sampler and two evaluators become one recorded episode. The reward is formed only at a terminal state.
- **Research role**: implements the Section 3.2 episodic MDP over $(\mathcal{S},\mathcal{A},\mathcal{R})$.
- **Equations**: the initial state, Equation (5), the transition and Equation (10):

$$
s_0=(\varnothing,\varnothing),\qquad \mathcal{A}(s_t)=(\mathcal{F}\setminus S_t)\cup\lbrace a_{\mathrm{stop}}\rbrace,\qquad S_{t+1}=S_t\cup\lbrace a\rbrace,\qquad f_{\mathrm{last}}=a.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: the stop action is the sentinel index $p$. Without a sampler, the deterministic reference rule takes the smallest available feature; that is a module contract, not the search policy.

### Task 10 — `run_task_10`

- **Inputs**: `p`, `config`, `heuristic_scores`, `beta`, `tau`.
- **Process**: allocates the table, zeroes it with `zero_uniform`, round-trips the boundary indices $\lbrace0,p-1,p\rbrace$, and evaluates the entropy of Equation (1) at the empty-state row.
- **Outputs**: `policy_table`, `shape`, `dtype`, `nbytes`, `init_seconds`, `initial_entropy`, `empty_state_row`, `initialization`, `verified`.
- **Transformation**: a universe size becomes an audited zero table of float64 weights.
- **Research role**: provides the policy matrix of Section 3.2 that captures pairwise feature synergies.
- **Equations**: with $W=0$ the initial policy reduces to the bias alone, so its entropy does not depend on $\tau$:

$$
W\in\mathbb{R}^{(p+1)\times(p+1)},\qquad \pi_0(a\mid s)=\frac{\exp(\beta_{s,a})}{\sum_{j}\exp(\beta_{s,a_j})},\qquad H_0=-\sum_a\pi_0(a\mid s)\ln\pi_0(a\mid s).
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: the paper describes a $p\times p$ matrix. The extra row is the empty state and the extra column is the stop action. Zero initialization is a study placeholder, not a manuscript statement.

### Task 11 — `run_task_11`

- **Inputs**: `X_train`, `y_train`, `feature_names`, `config`; optional `window_spec`, `epsilon`, `cache`, `fold_id`, `fold_train_end`, `validation_start`.
- **Process**: resolves the half-open estimation window, slices the matrices, computes relevance, and caches results under window-identifying keys.
- **Outputs**: the resolver fields, `relevance`, `zero_variance_mask`, `zero_variance_features`, `zero_variance_relevance`, `cache_key`, `cache_hit`, the fold fields, and both cache keys.
- **Transformation**: a training design and target become one absolute-correlation vector of length $p$.
- **Research role**: implements the relevance term of the Section 3.3 mRMR heuristic.
- **Equations**: on the estimation rows, with centred columns:

$$
\mathrm{Rel}(f_i)=|\rho(f_i,Y)|=\frac{\left|\sum_t(x_{it}-\bar x_i)(y_t-\bar y)\right|}{\lVert x_i-\bar x_i\rVert_2\,\lVert y-\bar y\rVert_2}.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: the paper leaves the correlation window open, so it is an explicit specification. In a fold context the window may not cross `fold_train_end` or `validation_start`, and the fold identifier enters the cache keys. Columns with standard deviation below `epsilon` receive relevance zero.

### Task 12 — `run_task_12`

- **Inputs**: `design_matrix`, `selected`; optional `candidates`, `configuration`, `k`, `window`, `correlation_matrix`.
- **Process**: resolves the variant, computes or accepts the correlation cache, then evaluates redundancy for each candidate.
- **Outputs**: `redundancy`, `variant`, `k`, `window`, `feature_names`, `correlation_matrix`, `masked_columns`.
- **Transformation**: a correlation matrix and a subset become one redundancy score per candidate.
- **Research role**: implements Equation (7) and the three metrics of Section 3.3 (fixed: mean; tuned: rms; endogenous: top-3-rms).
- **Equations**: with $c_{(m)}$ the $m$-th largest element of $\lbrace|\rho(f_i,f_j)|:f_j\in S\rbrace$:

$$
\mathrm{Red}_{\mathrm{top}\text{-}k\text{-rms}}(f_i,S)=\left(\frac{1}{\min(k,|S|)}\sum_{m=1}^{\min(k,|S|)}c_{(m)}^2\right)^{1/2}.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: `mean` averages $c_{(m)}^2$ with no root, as the paper states. An empty subset returns exactly 0.0. Numerically constant columns have their correlations set to zero and are listed in `masked_columns`.

### Task 13 — `run_task_13`

- **Inputs**: `relevance`, `selected`, `redundancy`, `config`; optional `configuration`, `policy_row`.
- **Process**: resolves $\gamma$, $\beta_{\mathrm{mag}}$ and $\tau$ for the Table 2 column, computes the bias, guards finiteness, and computes a softmax and the identification record.
- **Outputs**: `bias`, `complement_index`, `softmax_probabilities`, `beta_temperature_ratio`, `gamma`, `beta_mag`, `tau`, `configuration`, `parameter_sources`, `softmax_role`, `ratio_role`, `ratio_is_identified`, `identification`.
- **Transformation**: relevance and redundancy vectors become one additive bias over the ascending complement of $S$.
- **Research role**: implements Equation (8), the heuristic prior inside the GNRPA bias.
- **Equations**:

$$
\beta(f_i\mid S)=\beta_{\mathrm{mag}}\left[\gamma\,\mathrm{Rel}(f_i)-(1-\gamma)\,\mathrm{Red}(f_i,S)\right].
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: without `policy_row` the distribution is the heuristic prior, with $W=0$. Under Equation (1) only the weights are divided by $\tau$, so $\beta_{\mathrm{mag}}$ and $\tau$ are not identified through their ratio; `ratio_is_identified` is always false and the ratio is a diagnostic.

### Task 14 — `run_task_14`

- **Inputs**: `config`, `s_sizes`; optional `configuration_name`, `output_dir`.
- **Process**: resolves the Equation (9) block, evaluates the dispatched bias at each size, audits monotonicity on its grid, and audits the sentinel.
- **Outputs**: `has_fixed_cardinality`, `s_sizes`, `stop_biases`, `masked`, `monotone`, `grid`, `effective_capacity`, `sentinel`, `configuration_name`.
- **Transformation**: subset sizes become stopping biases and an effective rollout capacity.
- **Research role**: implements Equation (9), the endogenous stopping mechanism of Section 3.4.
- **Equations**:

$$
\beta(a_{\mathrm{stop}}\mid S)=\begin{cases}-\infty,&|S|<K_{\min}\\ \beta_{\mathrm{mag}}\,\gamma\,\beta_{\mathrm{stop}}\left(\dfrac{|S|-K_{\min}}{\max(1,K_{\max}-|S|)}\right)^{3/2},&|S|\ge K_{\min}.\end{cases}
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: $-\infty$ is the finite sentinel $-10^{30}$, audited to underflow to zero probability. The paper does not specify how fixed cardinality is enforced, so fixed and tuned runs use the placeholder hard mask $K_{\min}=K_{\max}=K$, with bias 0.0 at $|S|=K$.

### Task 15 — `run_task_15`

- **Inputs**: `policy_row`, `bias_vector`, `selected_features`, `n_features`, `temperature`, `rng`.
- **Process**: builds the legal mask, computes the softmax over legal actions, and draws one categorical action.
- **Outputs**: `mask`, `probabilities`, `action`, `action_probability`.
- **Transformation**: one policy row and bias vector become a normalised distribution and one action.
- **Research role**: implements the sampling line of Algorithm 1's rollout under Equations (1) and (5).
- **Equations**:

$$
\pi(a_k\mid s)=\frac{\exp\left(\dfrac{w_{s,a_k}}{\tau}+\beta_{s,a_k}\right)}{\sum_{a_j\in\mathcal{A}(s)}\exp\left(\dfrac{w_{s,a_j}}{\tau}+\beta_{s,a_j}\right)}.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: illegal positions are exactly zero and the stop action is always in the mask. The paper's figure says Gibbs sampling; the operation here is a single categorical draw, not a joint-conditional scheme.

### Task 16 — `run_task_16`

- **Inputs**: `feature_count`, `k_max`, `sample_action`, `evaluate_subset`; keyword `k_min`.
- **Process**: initialises the empty state, expands with the injected sampler until the stop action or capacity, and evaluates the complete terminal subset once.
- **Outputs**: `RolloutResultTask16`: the terminal state, trajectory, evaluator payload, terminal action and exit reason.
- **Transformation**: a sampling policy becomes one validated episode and its reward.
- **Research role**: implements the Rollout function of Algorithm 1.
- **Equations**: the loop runs while the stop action is unselected and capacity remains, then returns the terminal evaluation:

$$
\text{while}\ a\ne a_{\mathrm{stop}}\ \wedge\ |S|<K_{\max}:\quad a\sim\pi(\cdot\mid s),\quad s\leftarrow s\cup\lbrace a\rbrace;\qquad \text{return}\ \bigl(s,\mathcal{R}(S)\bigr).
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: the orchestrator never substitutes its own sampler. It proves that actions were legal, that the subset equals the union of feature actions, and that the exit reason agrees with $K_{\max}$.

### Task 17 — `terminal_fit_task_17`

- **Inputs**: `X_train`, `Y_train`, `subset_indices`; optional `stabilizer`, `condition_number_threshold`.
- **Process**: extracts the columns in ascending universe order, forms the normal-equation terms, and solves the stabilized system.
- **Outputs**: a dictionary of coefficients with the extraction and solve diagnostics.
- **Transformation**: a subset and training data become an unshrunk coefficient vector.
- **Research role**: implements the Section 3.2 estimator of Equation (6), which subset selection in Equation (4) wraps.
- **Equations**:

$$
\hat\theta_S=\left(X_{\mathrm{train},S}^\top X_{\mathrm{train},S}+\lambda I\right)^{-1}X_{\mathrm{train},S}^\top Y_{\mathrm{train}},\qquad \lambda=10^{-5}.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: $\lambda$ is a numerical device, not a statistical penalty, and there is no intercept. A condition number above $10^{12}$ is the warning threshold. A separate pseudoinverse kernel provides the minimum-norm solution.

### Task 18 — `run_task_18`

- **Inputs**: `X_train`, `X_val`, `y_val`, `feature_indices`, `theta_hat_S`, `study_config`.
- **Process**: sorts the subset once, permutes the coefficients identically, checks the column labels against training, predicts, scores, and summarises the distribution.
- **Outputs**: `predictions`, `r_squared`, `ss_res`, `ss_tot`, `score_dataset`, `r2_summary`, `subset_indices`, `theta_hat_S`.
- **Transformation**: terminal coefficients become a validation $R^2$ with an auditable column-to-coefficient pairing.
- **Research role**: implements the primary criterion of the terminal reward in Sections 3.2 and 3.4.
- **Equations**: the denominator uses the validation sample's own mean:

$$
\hat y_{\mathrm{val}}=X_{\mathrm{val},S}\hat\theta_S,\qquad R^2_{\mathrm{val}}=1-\frac{\sum_t(y_t-\hat y_t)^2}{\sum_t(y_t-\bar y_{\mathrm{val}})^2}.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: negative values are never clipped. A zero-variance target returns $-\infty$, which is excluded from the summary; `eq6_fit.score_dataset` must equal `internal_validation_562_days`.

### Task 19 — `run_task_19`

- **Inputs**: `X_val_sub`; optional `correlation_matrix_fit_on`, `singular_handling`, `singular_condition_threshold`.
- **Process**: validates the two placeholders, then computes the subset correlation matrix and its maximum inverse diagonal with size guards.
- **Outputs**: the compute-stage audit record, including `max_vif` and `raw_max_diagonal`.
- **Transformation**: a validation submatrix becomes one collinearity diagnostic.
- **Research role**: implements Equation (11), the tie-breaking criterion of Section 3.4.
- **Equations**:

$$
\mathrm{MaxVIF}(S)=\max_{1\le j\le|S|}\left(C_S^{-1}\right)_{jj}.
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: the paper does not fix the correlation sample, so `validation_subset` is a declared placeholder. Above a condition number of $10^{12}$ the Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse replaces the inverse. An empty subset returns zero diagnostics, and a negative diagonal is flagged.

### Task 20 — `run_task_20`

- **Inputs**: `candidates`, `output_dir`.
- **Process**: quantizes each primary key, forms each reward tuple, ranks the candidates and writes `reward_log.json`.
- **Outputs**: `records`, `ranking`, `best_index`, `best_reward`.
- **Transformation**: raw scores become ordered tuples, and rounding creates the ties that allow the later criteria to act.
- **Research role**: implements Equation (10), which orders accuracy, sparsity and collinearity without any weighted trade-off.
- **Equations**:

$$
\mathcal{R}(S)=\left(\lfloor R^2_{\mathrm{val}}(S)\rceil_3,\ -|S|,\ -\mathrm{MaxVIF}(S)\right).
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: rounding is half away from zero on the exact decimal representation, so binary floating-point artefacts cannot move a tie. The comparison is strict: equality and worsening both return false.

### Task 21 — `run_task_21`

- **Inputs**: `policy_weights`, `trajectory`, `config`, `level`, `tau`.
- **Process**: validates the table, routes $\alpha$ by recursion level, and replays the retained sequence once, recomputing Equation (1) at each state under the current table.
- **Outputs**: `policy_weights`, `n_updates`, `alpha`, `step_size`, `policies`, `level`, `configuration`, `tau`, `applied_state_keys`.
- **Transformation**: a retained trajectory becomes an updated policy table, with no value function or temporal-difference term.
- **Research role**: implements Equation (2) and the dual learning rates of Section 3.4 (for the endogenous row, 0.177 and 2.212).
- **Equations**:

$$
w_{s,a_j}\leftarrow w_{s,a_j}-\frac{\alpha}{\tau}\left(\pi(a_j\mid s)-\mathbb{I}_{\lbrace a_j=a^*\rbrace}\right),\qquad \alpha=\begin{cases}\alpha_{L1},&L=1\\ \alpha_{\mathrm{high}},&L>1.\end{cases}
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: only legal actions are updated, and illegal columns must stay byte-identical. Consecutive records with the same `state_key` are de-duplicated. The best action's weight must strictly increase whenever its probability is below one.

### Task 22 — `run_gnrpa_task_22`

- **Inputs**: `state`, `policy`, `config`, `rollout_fn`; optional `iteration_log`.
- **Process**: resolves and validates the Table 2 triple, patience, learning rates and temperature in a private configuration copy, enters the recursion at $L_{\mathrm{top}}=3$, and checks the return signature.
- **Outputs**: a tuple of the best sequence, a list of (state, action) pairs, and the length-three lexicographic reward.
- **Transformation**: a root state and the shared table become the best trajectory; deeper levels filter trajectories before any update.
- **Research role**: implements Algorithm 1, GNRPA with endogenous stopping.
- **Equations**: at each level $L\ge1$, with the update of Equation (2) applied toward the retained best sequence:

$$
(\mathrm{seq},\mathrm{score})\leftarrow\mathrm{GNRPA}(L-1,s,W),\qquad \text{break if}\ (L=1\wedge\mathrm{no\_improve}\ge P)\ \text{or}\ (L>1\wedge n\ge I_L).
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: the table is updated in place and shared across levels. The wrapper draws no random variates, so the injected rollout is the only stochastic component.

### Task 23 — `run_task_23`

- **Inputs**: `configuration`, `level_reward_traces`, `patience`, `output_dir`; keyword `level1_hard_cap`, `iteration_log`.
- **Process**: drives the root loop iteration by iteration through the live break predicate, runs each deeper level to its budget, measures per-level time, and audits a Task 22 log when supplied.
- **Outputs**: an audit with `configuration`, `patience`, `iteration_budgets`, `level1_iterations`, `level1_final_no_improve`, `levels`, `timing_seconds` and diagnostics.
- **Transformation**: reward traces become a verified termination record.
- **Research role**: implements the Section 3.4 patience rule, which makes $I_1$ variable, and the budgets of Table 2.
- **Equations**: strict lexicographic improvement resets the counter; ties and worsening increment it:

$$
\mathrm{no\_improve}\leftarrow\begin{cases}0,&\mathrm{score}>\mathrm{best\_score}\\ \mathrm{no\_improve}+1,&\text{otherwise.}\end{cases}
$$

- **Implementation distinction**: budgets are $84\times3\times6$, $18\times28\times18$ and var. $\times30\times15$, with $P=63$ for the endogenous row. Table 2 gives fixed and tuned no patience, so their root loops use $I_1$. An explicit hard cap is always enforced.

### Task 24 — `run_task_24`

- **Inputs**: `output_directory`; optional `configuration_names`.
- **Process**: instantiates each named Table 2 row, verifies every parameter against its declared literal, constructs each record twice, verifies the dual-alpha router, and writes the audit.
- **Outputs**: `configurations`, `audit_path`, `manuscript_targets`, `alpha_routing`.
- **Transformation**: declared literals become frozen records. No field depends on data or on a random draw.

### Task 25 — `run_task_25_baselines`

- **Inputs**: `X_train`, `y_train`, `X_test`, `y_test`, `config`, `output_dir`; optional `training_index`, `gnrpa_training_index`, `folds`, `X_history`, `y_history`, `validation_length`, `strict_convergence`.

$$
\min_{\beta}\frac{1}{2n}\lVert y_c-X_c\beta\rVert_2^2+\lambda\lVert\beta\rVert_1,\qquad \min_{b_0,b}\lVert y-b_0-Xb\rVert_2^2+\alpha\lVert b\rVert_2^2.

- **Implementation distinction**: Equation (6) does not apply here, and these baselines carry an intercept. The paper does not specify scaling, so `baselines.standardization` selects it; with `training_window_z_score` the training moments alone are used and coefficients are reported in raw units.

### Task 26 — `run_task_26`
